C 3.7 MIN  = minimal direction prompt, single forward/back request, LOW / LOW / LOW.
D 3.7 FULL = current full path, LOW / LOW / LOW.
E 3.8 MIN  = minimal direction prompt, single forward/back request, LOW / LOW / LOW.
F 3.8 FULL = current full path, LOW / LOW / LOW.
Common post-evaluation: Critical detector + Script contamination + same 6-axis Semantic Judge. These do not alter generation or measured forward/back path.
Interpretation: same-model MIN→FULL isolates app-configuration effect; MIN-vs-MIN isolates model difference; FULL-vs-FULL measures practical replacement effect.
